Living Your Role: What is an RPG?

Role-Playing Games (RPGs) give players detailed control over a single character's growth, class choices, equipment builds, and storyline decisions. The core mechanics center on earning experience points (XP) to unlock traits and optimize gear layouts.

Take Judgement the Masked Knight as an example. Starting as a simple squire, players complete main quests, select attributes (like Gothic Spellcasting or Physical Defense), and defeat gothic bosses to forge legendary masks containing ancient power.

  • Character Customization: Allocate talent points to build a pure mage, a heavy shield knight, or a hybrid.
  • Deep Item Economy: Hunt rare loot packs, slot magical runes into swords, and upgrade gear.
  • Quest-Driven Narrative: Align with varying factions and alter the fate of gothic castles with your decisions.
