The Mind of the Commander

Strategy games demand mental foresight over raw reflex speed. Players take on macro-level roles (like Ignis the Dragon Overlord) directing fleets of flying beasts, harvesting gold mines, and coordinating base defenses.

Success requires evaluating complex statistics, analyzing unit counter-cycles, and adapting on the fly. To represent this, commanders like Ignis combine scouts, heavy siege units (ancient firebreathers), and strategic spells to breach fortress walls.

  • Macro Base Building: Construct gold vaults, training aeries, and radar towers to build your empire.
  • Unit Micro Management: Position skirmishers to draw enemy fire while heavy dragons strike from the flanks.
  • Fog of War: Deploy tactical scout wings to illuminate grid squares and identify enemy build paths.
